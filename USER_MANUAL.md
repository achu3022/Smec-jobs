# SMECJobs User Manual

Welcome to SMECJobs! This manual provides an overview of how to use the platform depending on your account type: **Jobseeker**, **Employer**, or **Administrator**.

---

## 1. For Jobseekers (Students)

The Jobseeker portal is designed to help you build your profile, find the right courses, and apply for jobs effortlessly.

### Setting Up Your Profile
1. **Log in** to your account.
2. Navigate to your **Dashboard**. By default, it opens on your **My Profile** tab.
3. Keep your **Profile Completion** high by adding your Location, Experience, and Skills.
4. Go to the **Resume** tab to upload your CV. A complete profile increases your chances of getting hired.

### Finding & Applying for Jobs
1. Click on **Find a Job** in the top navigation bar.
2. Use the search bar and filters to find roles that match your skills.
3. Click **Apply** on a job listing. You can track all your applications in the **Applied Jobs** tab on your Dashboard.

### Managing Interviews & Notifications
- **Interviews Tab**: If an employer schedules an interview with you, it will appear here.
- **Messages Tab**: Communicate directly with employers regarding your applications.
- **Notifications Tab**: Alerts about application status changes or interview schedules will appear here. They are organized by Unread and Recent.

---

## 2. For Employers (Companies)

The Employer portal allows companies to post jobs, manage applicants, and find top talent.

### Setting Up Your Company
1. **Log in** with an Employer account.
2. Navigate to **Company Profile** in your sidebar.
3. Fill out your company details, logo, website, and description. This information is visible to candidates when they view your job postings.

### Posting & Managing Jobs
1. Click **Post a Job** to create a new listing. Fill out the title, description, requirements, and salary range.
2. Go to **Manage Jobs** to view all your active and closed listings. You can edit them, duplicate them, or mark them as urgently hiring.

### Reviewing Applicants & Talent Pool
1. Navigate to the **Applicants** tab to see everyone who has applied to your jobs.
2. Use the **Kanban Board** or Data Table to move candidates through the hiring pipeline (e.g., Shortlisted, Interviewing, Hired, Rejected).
3. If you find a candidate you like but aren't ready to hire, click the **Heart Icon** to save them to your **Talent Pool** (Saved Candidates tab) for future reference.

### Analytics & Reports
- **Dashboard**: View high-level metrics like Total Applications, Active Jobs, and pipeline charts.
- **Reports Tab**: View detailed performance data for every job you've posted, including conversion rates. You can **Export as CSV** for offline analysis.

---

## 3. For Administrators

The Admin portal provides full control over the platform's data and configuration.

### Managing the Ecosystem
- **Users**: View, edit, or delete Jobseekers and Employers.
- **Companies**: Monitor and verify company profiles.
- **Jobs**: Oversee all job postings. You can feature specific jobs so they appear at the top of search results, or remove inappropriate listings.
- **Courses & Enquiries**: Manage the educational courses offered on the platform and track user enquiries.

### Analytics & Export
- The **Admin Dashboard** provides system-wide analytics, including registration trends and global application distribution.
- The **Reports & Export** tab allows you to export full database records (Users, Jobs, Applications) to CSV for external audits and reporting.

### Global Settings
- Navigate to **Settings** to update the platform's global parameters (e.g., Contact Email, Phone, Social Media links). These settings dynamically update the footer and contact pages across the entire site.
